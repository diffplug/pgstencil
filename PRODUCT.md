# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

TypeScript/pnpm libraries published to npm: Postgres, IntegreSQL, Kysely, Better Auth, Stripe, Hono, Vitest and Supertest. The example application is a JSON API with a minimal page, on Node or Cloudflare Workers.

## Users

Developers building Postgres websites who want sign-in and subscriptions they can test deterministically, and the people who sign in to and pay for those websites.

## Product Purpose

Make real-database tests parallel, readable and deterministic through isolated databases, server time, seeded randomness and captured email, and supply audited sign-in and billing built the same way.

## Capabilities and Constraints

README.md, PACKAGES.md, BILLING.md and SECURITY.md are the product brief. The packages are libraries: applications own their pages, branding and routes. The example exists to exercise and demonstrate the packages, not as a designed product.

## Brand Commitments

Name: pgstencil. No visual assets or commercial claims.
